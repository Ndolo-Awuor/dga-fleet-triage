Transformer dissolved-gas records and the diagnosis to be produced
=================================================================

Fleet: 24 oil-filled power transformers (mineral oil), T01-T24, sampled roughly twice a year from 2012.
The records are synthetic (generated by a seeded program); every convention below is part of the task.

Files (all in /app/data)
------------------------
samples.csv  unit, date (YYYY-MM-DD), H2, CH4, C2H6, C2H4, C2H2, CO, CO2
             Dissolved-gas concentrations in ppm (microlitres of gas per litre of oil), as reported by the laboratory.
units.csv    unit, oil_type, commissioned_year, region
events.csv   unit, date, event, detail
               top_up     oil added to the unit; detail = percentage of the oil volume added. The added oil is gas-free,
                          so the dissolved-gas concentrations fall by that fraction; gas generation is not affected.
               degassing  vacuum degassing. Removes most of the dissolved gas; a few percent remains. Generation is not affected.

Laboratories
------------
Samples were analysed by contract laboratories over the life of the record. Express every rate below in the
measurement scale used by each unit's most recent samples.

What to produce
---------------
/app/diagnosis.json - one entry per unit:

  {"T01": {"condition": "benign", "action": "routine"}, "T02": {...}, ...}

condition (one of):
  benign             no ongoing fault gas generation. Includes units with high but stable gas levels, for example
                     oil-related ("stray") gassing that has levelled off.
  thermal            ongoing generation dominated by CH4, C2H6 and C2H4.
  partial_discharge  ongoing generation dominated by H2.
  arcing             ongoing generation that includes acetylene (C2H2), with H2 and C2H4.

action (one of): routine, increase_sampling, investigate, remove_from_service

Generation rate and the action rule (this task's own convention, not a published standard)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The generation rate is the average rate at which a unit has been producing gas over the last 3 years of the record, in
ppm per year, in latest-laboratory units. It is generation, not the change in concentration: dilution by a top-up or a
degassing does not reduce it. rate_sum is the combined rate of H2, CH4, C2H6, C2H4 and C2H2.

  benign unit                                       -> routine
  unit with a fault, and either
      rate_sum above 250 ppm/yr, or C2H2 rate above 5 ppm/yr   -> remove_from_service
      rate_sum above 60 ppm/yr,  or C2H2 rate above 1 ppm/yr   -> investigate
      otherwise                                                -> increase_sampling
